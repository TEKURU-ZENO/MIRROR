# ADR-001: Vertical Selection — Critical Infrastructure Incident Assessment

- **Status**: Accepted & Locked
- **Date**: 2026-11-09
- **Author**: MIRROR Architecture Team

## Context
Field inspection systems often attempt broad generic form-filling across retail or residential real estate, which fails to capture the high-consequence, multi-agent complexity required by heavy civil infrastructure. In disasters (wildfires, hurricanes, earthquakes), power grids and bridges experience cascading failures where offline edge reliability, cryptographic evidence chain-of-custody, and licensed engineering accountability are life-or-death requirements.

## Decision
MIRROR is purposefully built for **Critical Utility and Civil Infrastructure Incident Assessment** (high-voltage substations, transmission corridors, transformers, and bridge piers).

## Consequences
- Requires strict spatial graph modeling for topological cascade detection.
- Demands high-assurance evidence integrity and deterministic citation grounding.
- Enforces NERC-CIP and DOT compliance readiness.
